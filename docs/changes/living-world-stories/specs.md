# Required behavior

- F3+G and owner `/ecology info` show the region, species total/capacity, represented
  bodies, the stored three daily changes, known nearby nest and burn history.
  Information reads never create a region, refresh field observations, advance
  a cursor, assign an ID or dirty terrain. Unknown/unobserved state is explicit.
- `/ecology advance <1-60>` retains the same host-owned day sequence as ordinary
  simulation; guests are refused before model lookup. Supported OFF/ON and
  protected newer data retain existing policy.
- Story A links real predator losses and increased rabbits to an ordinary crop
  raid. A closed barrier protects a second plot; opening it permits a meal.
- Story B links a real nest, scheduled silk, finite night bodies and destruction
  to founding in another eligible dark cave. Cleared hearts do not emit again.
- Story C links consumed fire fuel, rain/ash, scar dates, grass/natural trees and
  ordinary finite materialization. Actual traffic creates a trail that fades
  after abandonment. No successful forest or returned body is authored manually.
- Common saves retain these features, plant schedules, animal/nest reservations
  and unique IDs together, including return after 30 calendar days away.
- Matching LAN and NORMAL-server clients see host terrain, silk/nests, trails,
  crop stages and finite bodies through ordinary existing snapshots. The
  dedicated snapshot remains valid after `/stop`.
