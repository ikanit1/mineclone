# Finite wildlife (LW-01, LW-02, LW-03, LW-10)

Hunting previously removed a body, then the spawner refilled its category from nowhere.
Living World gives seven animal species a finite population per 256×256 region.
Births, wolves, neighbouring habitats and the player's kills change that population.
The owner can inspect it and advance bounded days through `/ecology`.

The rule remains opt-in until LW-13. Ordinary spawning stays on its previous path
when it is off. Spider nests, field raids and forest regrowth follow in later cards.
